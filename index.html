My take, under every story

Promise Robotics raises CA$40M to sell factory capacity to homebuilders

Promise takes a builder's existing designs, runs them through its robotic plants in Alberta and sells the output. It has more than 2,500 homes under contract, and D.R. Horton has signed on.

My take

Forget the funding round. What I'd watch is that builders get the homes without ever running a plant.

University of Arkansas fuses simulated moon dust into LEGO-like bricks

A laser turns lunar regolith simulant into interlocking blocks a few centimeters wide, with no binder. They stand on their own but can't carry a load yet.

My take

Tiny, and untested in vacuum. Still, a brick with no glue is a decent first step for building on the Moon.

Paper · MIT CBA · Aug 2026

Robots that build reconfigurable structures from self-aligning lattice modules

Modules whose geometry gives robots surfaces to grasp and self-align, with snap-fit connectors so structures can be taken apart and rebuilt.

Limit: stiff lattices that rely on connectors rather than structural form.

Hi, I'm Chi.

I'm an architectural designer who spent the last few years around robot arms, 3D printers and computational design in Barcelona: Robotics and Advanced Construction at IAAC, and the MADE master's at UPC.

I started UN/BUILT because news about construction robots is usually written for investors. I want to know what it changes for the people who design and build. Claims link to primary sources, simulations are labelled as simulations, and other people's work is credited.
